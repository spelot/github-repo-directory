## Risk notes

SCRAPES login-walled platforms (X, Reddit, LinkedIn search, XiaoHongShu, Facebook, Instagram) by copying your Chrome logins (cookie reuse) into its own headless browser; README itself says platforms may restrict accounts and recommends a secondary account. Breaks X/Reddit/LinkedIn/Meta automated-access terms. Maintainer promises to work around anti-bot changes; sponsors are scraping/proxy vendors. Read-only (no posting). The no-login defaults (GitHub, YouTube, web) are lower risk. Never enable login channels on your main accounts. Install only from the GitHub install.md, not the PyPI 'agent-reach' package.
